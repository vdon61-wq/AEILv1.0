# AEIL Trademark and Branding Policy

**Status:** Project policy for AEIL v1.0

This document governs use of the names **AEIL**, **AI Emotional Intelligence Layer**,
related logos, badges, and certification terminology associated with the project.

Copyright licenses for the AEIL specification or code do not automatically grant
trademark rights.

## Permitted descriptive use

You may truthfully use wording such as:

- “Implements AEIL v1.0”
- “Compatible with AEIL v1.0”
- “Based on the AEIL v1.0 specification”
- “Uses the AEIL v1.0 benchmark”

provided that the statement is accurate and does not imply official approval,
certification, endorsement, or affiliation.

## Restricted use

Without explicit authorization, do not use AEIL branding in a way that suggests:

- the AEIL project has certified your product;
- your product has passed an official AEIL certification process;
- the AEIL project endorses your company, model, agent, product, or service;
- your organization is the official AEIL organization;
- a modified specification is the official AEIL specification.

The terms **“AEIL Certified”**, **“Official AEIL Certified”**, **“AEIL Approved”**,
and similar certification or approval claims should be reserved for organizations
authorized under the applicable certification program.

## Logos and badges

If official AEIL logos or badges are published, their use may be subject to additional
brand guidelines. Do not alter official marks in a misleading manner or incorporate them
into a product identity in a way that creates confusion about affiliation.

## Compatibility statements

A compatibility statement should identify the exact version where practical:

> Tested against AEIL v1.0.

This is preferable to an unqualified statement such as “AEIL compliant” when the
implementation has not undergone a defined conformity assessment.

## Policy changes

The project may publish updated branding and certification policies for future AEIL
versions. Users should identify the policy version applicable to their use.

**Note:** This document is a project policy template, not a substitute for trademark
registration, legal advice, or jurisdiction-specific trademark analysis.
