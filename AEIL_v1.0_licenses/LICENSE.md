# AEIL v1.0 — Licensing Overview

AEIL uses a **multi-license structure** because the specification, benchmark, software,
branding, and certification program have different legal and operational roles.

| Material | License / Policy |
|---|---|
| Specification and prose documentation | CC BY 4.0 |
| Public benchmark | CC BY 4.0 |
| Code, schemas and implementation utilities | Apache-2.0 |
| AEIL name, logos and branding | TRADEMARK.md |
| Certification and certification marks | CERTIFICATION.md |

## Why separate licenses?

The goal is to keep AEIL open for research, implementation, commercial products, and
independent integrations while preserving clear control over official branding and
certification claims.

Commercial use is intentionally permitted. The open license does not require a company
to make its own product open source merely because it implements AEIL.

## Attribution

Recommended attribution:

> Based on AEIL v1.0 — AI Emotional Intelligence Layer.

Where possible, identify the exact AEIL specification and benchmark versions used.

## Certification

Downloading, implementing, or passing the public benchmark does not automatically grant
certification. Certification is a separate assessment process governed by
`CERTIFICATION.md`.

## Legal review

These files are intended as a practical open-source/open-standard licensing architecture
for the project. Before commercial launch, trademark registration, certification
services, or distribution in multiple jurisdictions, obtain a review from qualified
intellectual-property/open-source counsel.
